# Tribes and register

**One tribe per script.** The archetype set is not tribe-neutral, and a script
that praises a stanced car for its lap times was written by someone who read
about both and understood neither.

## The organising insight

**Every automotive tribe polices the same sin — appearance outrunning function —
but each defines "function" differently.** That one frame generates all the
contempt in the niche. Stance is the deliberate exception, which is exactly why
it draws the most cross-tribe hostility.

## Car-side

| Tribe | Respects | Holds in contempt | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **JDM / tuner** | Chassis- and engine-code literacy, swaps done properly, tuning with datalogs | Cosmetic mods imitating speed; non-functional wings, fart-can exhausts | AE86, EK9, S13/S14/S15, FD3S, JZA80; 2JZ-GTE, RB26DETT, 4G63, K24, 13B-REW; "single turbo," "built vs boosted." **Avoid "ricer"** — dated and racially loaded; using it casually marks you as out of touch |
| **Stance / fitment** | Fitment precision, camber/offset math, event pedigree | "Function-only" people as joyless; poke/stretch done badly | Offset/ET, poke, stretch, tucking, air-over-static. "Hellaflush" is dated. **Inverts the function test on purpose — see the warning below** |
| **Track / time-attack** | Seat time, data, consistency, brake and tire management | Horsepower with no lap times; mall-dyno numbers | Apex, trail braking, brake bias, tire temps hot vs cold, sector times. "Slow in, fast out" is a cliché to them |
| **Classic / restoration** | Numbers-matching, correct date codes, hand fabrication | Trailer queens. Restomod vs originality is a live internal fight | Numbers-matching, date-coded, survivor, barn find, concours, driver-quality |
| **Truck / overland** | Actual trail use, recovery competence, knowing your own build | Unused capability. Verbatim tells: "nerf bars instead of real sliders," "more lip than sidewall," "a lift with 20-inch rims and it's 2wd," and the kill shot — not knowing your own lift: *"Not really sure, I took it into the shop and it looks rad"* | Rock sliders vs nerf bars, recovery points vs tie-downs, spacer lift vs proper lift, departure angle, aired-down, mall crawler |
| **Euro / VAG** | Servicing knowledge, timing chain and carbon build-up literacy | Badge inflation (M badge on a 328i), leased-and-tuned | E30, E46, F80, B8, MK7; "carbon clean," DSG vs DCT. **Bimmer = BMW car. Beemer = BMW motorcycle.** |
| **American muscle** | Cubic inches, drag times, drivetrain durability | Cosmetic muscle. "Mustang leaving cars and coffee" jokes are exhausted | 60-foot time, ET, trap speed, cam specs, LS/LT swap, Coyote, solid roller |
| **EV / tech** | Instrumented data, charge-curve behaviour, real-condition efficiency | Vibes-based range claims; fanboy *and* hater posturing alike | kWh/100mi, mi/kWh, C-rate, taper, preconditioning, SoC, DCFC, one-pedal, 800V, LFP vs NMC |
| **Exotic / lifestyle** | Access, rarity knowledge, actually driving the thing | Implied ownership of rented or borrowed cars | — |
| **Consumer buyer** | Plain numbers, dealer-process specifics, money saved | **Enthusiast gatekeeping aimed at them.** A different audience entirely | Out-the-door price, doc fee, dealer add-ons, money factor, residual, buy rate vs marked-up rate |

## Moto-side

| Tribe | Respects | Holds in contempt | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sportbike** | Body position, corner entry, track days | Squids — inadequate gear *plus* speed *plus* showing off *plus* thin skill | Clip-ons, rearsets, trail braking, knee-down, hanging off |
| **Cruiser / Harley** | Miles, wrenching, club and ride history | Metric-cruiser posturing; being lectured by sportbike riders. **Largest paying moto demographic** | Bagger, brain bucket, stage kit, "the wave," V-twin cadence |
| **ADV** | Actual mileage, self-recovery, load management | The bike that never leaves pavement — "the guy whose weekend goal is a good parking spot in front of Starbucks" | Farkle, panniers, standing on the pegs, tank slap |
| **Dirt / supermoto** | Bike control, jump technique | Street-only posers on knobbies | Roost, whiskey throttle, holeshot, scrub, dab |
| **Vintage / café** | Getting old machinery running and keeping it running | — ("a holocaust of 1970s crap bikes" is the affectionate insider joke about the scene itself) | The ton, ton-up, clip-ons, points, jetting, kick-only |
| **Touring** | Comfort engineering, route knowledge, mileage | Speed-first content | Farkle also lives here; saddle time, wind management |

## Vocabulary discipline — hard rule

These terms are **not one register.** *Farkle* is ADV/touring. *The ton* is
British café. *Brain bucket* is old-school cruiser. *Roost* is dirt.
*Clip-ons/rearsets* is sportbike/café.

> **Three of them in one script is the loudest possible tourist signal — worse
> than using none.**

Maximum **one** tribe-specific term per script unless Sam is demonstrably native
to that tribe.

## Cross-tribe collisions to check before writing

- **Stance is structurally excluded** by nearly every archetype in this skill.
  The dominant voices are all function-over-form and stance inverts that test
  deliberately. If the audience is show/fitment, say so rather than writing
  hostile material anyway.
- **Cruiser/Harley audiences reject the contrarian-tech-critic register.** It is
  sportbike/ADV-coded with open contempt for cruiser orthodoxy.
- **ADV, touring and cruiser read sportbike skill demos as squid behaviour.**
- **Consumer-buyer audiences are people buying a car, not enthusiasts.**
  Applying engine-code literacy to them is a category error — and this is the
  audience attached to the single largest verified Short in the corpus.
- **Patina is a landmine.** Romanticised by the revive-and-drive audience;
  neglect or a defect to a restoration or auction audience. Route on Sam's
  tribe, not on the aesthetic.

## Market register — lock before line one

The reference corpus mixes UK, Canadian, US and Australian creators freely. An
unlocked agent will write "the bonnet" into a US script.

| | US | UK | CA | AU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panel | hood / trunk | bonnet / boot | hood / trunk | bonnet / boot |
| Rubber | tire | tyre | tire | tyre |
| Currency | $ USD | £ | $ CAD | $ AUD |
| Fuel | gas, mpg (US) | petrol, mpg (imp) | gas, L/100km | petrol, L/100km |

Never mix. Never use a regional in-joke outside its market. **Hard field, no
default** — it lives in `voice/profile.md`.
