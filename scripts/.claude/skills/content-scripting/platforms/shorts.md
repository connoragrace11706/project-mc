# YouTube Shorts

## Mechanics  <!-- ROTS FAST — re-verify quarterly -->

**The load-bearing sentence, verbatim from Help** [T1]: "Our systems use the
signals for % of viewers who chose to view, avg. view duration and avg. %
viewed to inform ranking," plus "likes and post-watch survey results" as
enjoyment gauges.

**Comments and shares are not named in that list.** Absence from one help page
is not proof of exclusion — YouTube's docs are deliberately partial — but it is
a meaningful difference from TikTok, where comment probability is an explicit
predicted interaction. **Practical effect: comment hooks are worth less here
than on TikTok, and sends are worth much less than on Reels.**

The Shorts tab ranks "based on their performance and relevancy to that
individual viewer," personalising on which Shorts and channels the viewer
enjoyed before, topics they watch, and sounds they engaged with [T1].

**Evaluation is per-video, not per-channel** [T1]: "Experimenting with new
content formats... will not inherently confuse the algorithm or negatively
impact a channel's overall performance," and "The system evaluates each piece of
content individually." **A format experiment costs nothing structurally.**

**The metric trap that changes everything** [T1]. Since **31 March 2025**, a
Shorts *View* counts the moment a video starts to play **or replays**, with no
minimum watch time. *Engaged views* is "How many times viewers stayed to watch
past the initial seconds, **not including any loops.**" **YPP eligibility and
Shorts ad revenue share both run on Engaged views.**

Consequence: **loops inflate Views and earn nothing.** Loops are a payoff
device, not a distribution device. Any statistic whose denominator spans 31 Mar
2025 is measuring a definitional change, not audience change.

**No benchmark exists for Viewed vs Swiped Away** [T1, absence verified].
YouTube shipped the metric with zero interpretive guidance, and it is confounded
by traffic source — a Short surfaced to a well-targeted audience shows a high
viewed rate for reasons unrelated to quality. **A universal threshold is not a
coherent construct even in principle. Compare against the distribution of your
own recent Shorts.**

**Timing is not a lever** [T2, Todd Sherman, Director of Shorts PM]: publishing
time is irrelevant unless covering news; quality over quantity; delete-and-
reupload can be flagged. The "first 30–60 minutes decides everything" model is
imported from long-form subscriber-feed dynamics the Shorts feed does not share.

**YouTube publishes unusually concrete creative guidance** [T1, 16 Apr 2025 and
14 Jul 2026]:
- Open with "a question, a surprising fact, or a visually captivating moment"
- "Get to the point quickly"
- "Shorts are often watched without sound, so captions are essential"
- Crop to 9:16
- Build verbal *and* visual cues during the final 5 seconds
- **"The first 5 to 10 seconds of your long video should directly address the
  topic, question, or hook promised in the Short."**
- Trending audio is explicitly **not** required: "Original content and unique
  ideas succeed without trending audio"

## Culture  <!-- SEMI-DURABLE -->

Lean-back velocity. Muted, thumbnail-adjacent, no relational layer. Closer to a
television channel than to a conversation, which is why comment hooks and send
triggers both underperform relative to the other two platforms.

Automotive fit is unusually good: the instructional archetypes that dominate
this niche map cleanly onto "% viewers who chose to view" and onto YouTube's own
"answer a question quickly" guidance. The largest verified Shorts in the niche
corpus are almost all how-to.

## Scripting rules

- **The first beat must read as a complete proposition on a muted,
  thumbnail-sized frame.** Use one of YouTube's three named devices: a question,
  a surprising fact, or a visually captivating moment.
- **Open loop in sentence 1, closed in the last.** Average % viewed is a named
  ranking input; the viewer needs a stated reason to reach the end. **This is
  the opposite of Reels** — do not reuse a front-loaded Reel script here.
- **Burn captions.** Non-optional; YouTube says so directly.
- **A loop seam is a payoff device, not a growth device.** Loops inflate Views
  and earn nothing. Do not optimise for them.
- **CTA in the final 5 seconds, verbal *and* visual.**
- If a long-form version exists, set the Related Video in Studio and make sure
  the long-form's first 5–10 seconds directly address the Short's hook.
- **Format experiments are free** — evaluation is per-video, so a new archetype
  cannot damage the channel.
- Instructional framing is a strength here, not a compromise. Short imperative,
  or a physical question with a real answer. **Never a multi-clause
  course-catalogue title.**

## Degradation order

1. Cut connective tissue
2. Cut the second demonstration angle
3. Tighten the setup, never the payoff

**Never cut:** the muted-frame proposition, the closing of the opened loop, the
burned captions, or the final-5-seconds CTA.

## Refuted — do not restate, and correct Sam if he asserts these

- **"70%+ Viewed-vs-Swiped-Away is viral, below 30% dies."** YouTube shipped the
  metric with zero interpretive guidance, and traffic-source confounding makes a
  universal threshold incoherent. The thresholds also drift between sources
  while gaining precision — the signature of generated numbers.
- **"Distribution stops if it doesn't clear a threshold in 30–60 minutes."** No
  first-party basis; contradicted by Sherman on timing.
- **The "three-gate seed batch."** Invented.
- **"Tubular's 2025 report found under-30% swipe-away gets 4x sustained
  distribution."** No such finding exists, and swipe-away is a creator-private
  Studio metric with no public API — a panel firm could not compute it.
- **Every subscriber-conversion benchmark** in circulation.
- **Any raw *Views* comparison spanning 31 Mar 2025.** The definition changed;
  the number is not comparable to itself.
