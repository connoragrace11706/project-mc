# Script structure

## The shape

```
FOOTHOLD + GAP        0.0 – 2.0s     domain established, tension opened
FIRST PAYMENT         3.0 – 8.0s     partial resolution; opens the next gap
BODY                  varies         alternating payment and new gap
FINAL PAYMENT         before close   the thing promised, delivered
CLOSE                 last 2s        loop seam, hard stop, or CTA
```

Payoff placement varies by platform and the variation is not cosmetic — see
`curiosity-debt.md`. Reels front-loads. Shorts closes the loop at the end. They
are opposites.

## Two layers, always

For every video script, write two deliverables that each work alone:

**The muted layer.** A pinned text card, top third, high contrast, static
through the opening. It states a **complete claim.** Persistent, not animated in
and swapped out. The single most useful structural finding in the niche research
is that the mute problem is solved by a pinned card, **not by subtitles** —
verified against multiple 500K–1.4M automotive Shorts with no speech track at
all.

**The sound layer.** Adds a *distinct* reward for unmuting. In automotive this
is often the entire product: the engine note, the exhaust, the downshift, the
turbo. "Use the audio from a reel you're viewing" and "tap the soundtrack" are
separately modelled prediction actions on Reels and TikTok respectively [T1].

**Never write a card that is a transcript of the voiceover.** The one deliberate
exception is the reaction/breakdown format, where reading the banner aloud is
the style.

Layout: claim in the upper third, numbers or countdown in the lower third,
action clear in the middle. Stacked variants build line by line rather than
replacing.

## Title and overlay carry different information

They must not duplicate. Observed in this niche: title = "He won't make it far"
(outcome tease), overlay = "I RAN OVER HIS CHAIN" (cause). Duplicating them
wastes the second-best piece of real estate you own.

## Openers

Winning on-camera openers in this niche begin **mid-thought**, on a conjunction
or a filler: *"and a lot of people say..."*, *"so this lady with her Nissan, uh,
oil everywhere"*, *"well I wasn't going to make any more shorts but..."*. Do not
clean this up; over-polished delivery reads as marketing.

**Critical distinction — do not blur this.** A mid-sentence cold open is
legitimate when it is Sam's own continuous take or genuine incidental
documentary audio. **It is a fabrication instruction when the agent writes a
sentence for a third party's voice and cuts into it as if captured.** Never do
that.

**Self-introduction is an anti-pattern** for anyone without established brand
equity. The one creator observed doing it successfully can pay the branding tax
because the brand *is* the promise.

## Closes

| Platform | Close |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Loop seam (last frame flows into first) or hard stop |
| Reels | Send prompt after the payoff, spoken and on-screen, naming a real relationship |
| Shorts | CTA in the final 5 seconds with **verbal and visual** cue [T1]; route to long-form via the Related Video tool in Studio, not a description link |

Never a trailing "anyway, thanks for watching." Dead tails are measured.

## Degradation order

When a constraint bites, the platform file declares what gets cut and in what
order, so the cut is never improvised. Generic order:

1. Connective tissue
2. The second example
3. Merge two micro-payoffs into one

**Never cut:** the frame-1 state, the muted card's claim, the loop seam, or any
payoff for a gap you already opened.
