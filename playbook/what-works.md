# What Works

Confirmed patterns only. A pattern enters this file after holding across **3+
posts**. Every entry cites the posts it came from and the date it was confirmed.

If you can't cite the evidence, it belongs in `hypotheses.md` instead.

---

## Format

```
### <Pattern name>
- **Confirmed:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Evidence:** <post ids / titles, and the numbers>
- **The pattern:** <what to actually do>
- **Why it likely works:** <mechanism — retention, curiosity gap, relatability>
- **Where it applies:** <platform / format / pillar>
```

---

*Empty. Nothing is confirmed until we have two snapshots and three posts
pointing the same direction. Starting assumptions live in `hypotheses.md`
where they belong — including the ones that feel obvious.*
