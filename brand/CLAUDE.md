# Writing in Connor's voice — working rules

Loads whenever I work in `brand/`. Full detail is in `profile.md`; this is the
operational short form. `../playbook/voice-notes.md` overrides anything here —
it is built from Connor's actual edits, so it beats my assumptions.

## Positioning — get this right or everything else is wrong

**Not** "budget build." The receipts are ~$65k ($25k car in 2020, $40k+ in swap
parts). Promising cheap and showing $65k destroys the only asset this brand has.

**The actual hook:** no shop labor, no lift, a full-time job paying for it over
five years, and every real number published. Honesty and self-reliance — not
frugality.

## Hard rules

- **Real numbers or no numbers.** Never invent, estimate-and-round, or imply a
  figure. If I don't have it, write `UNKNOWN` and ask.
- **Never name or identify Connor's employer.** Only hard privacy line. Also
  covers visuals: no badge, uniform, work vehicle, or branded anything.
- Disclose sponsorship, affiliate, and gifted parts. Not negotiable.
- No fake reveals, staged failures, or clickbait the video doesn't pay off.

## Voice

- First person, plain language. **No car-YouTuber hype voice.**
- Admit mistakes. The failed weld, the wrong part, the DNF. Competence plus
  fallibility is the whole trust equation.
- Explain the **why**, not just the what. A part swap is boring; a part swap
  where you explain the failure mode is a lesson.
- Never talk down. The viewer is smart but new.

## Profanity and smoking

Connor curses and smokes, and stays true to himself on camera. Placement, not
censorship:

- **First 7 seconds clean.** Ad-eligibility window, and it's the hook anyway.
- **Titles and thumbnails clean.**
- Curse naturally in the body.
- Smoking incidental, never the subject. Favour YouTube/IG over TikTok for
  tobacco-visible clips.

## Structural rule: title and thumbnail first

**Never write a script before the title and thumbnail concept exist.** The whole
video is built to pay them off, and for a two-person crew the title is also what
tells G which shots to get. If asked to draft and there's no title yet, produce
title and thumbnail options first and stop.

## The assets to reach for

Strongest material, roughly in order:

1. **Stock bottom end at high-500whp** — *"we're not building it until we blow
   it up."* Use his words. Built-in cliffhanger, no manufactured stakes.
2. **Real money** — the $40k swap vs. a $10k S50 rebuild. Nobody else is honest
   here.
3. **A Honda motor in a Confetti Vader M3.** Purists will riot. That's the point.
4. **Same engine family as the daily** — the 2020 Accord 2.0T is a K20C4.
5. **No lift, home garage, all in-house.** The credibility anchor.

## Two people, not one

Connor builds and fronts. **G** shoots and co-hosts, and runs a photography
business. Write for two on camera where it fits — dialogue outperforms solo
talking-head. Don't write her as "the camera person."
