## The gap — what's still needed for the $40k segment

This is a **manifest, not an invoice list.** Of roughly 90 line items, **five
have prices**, totalling $1,321 — about **3%** of the stated $40,000+.

The video's strongest four minutes is *"here's what $40,000 actually buys,"* and
it does not work without numbers. Per the hard line: **real numbers or no
numbers.**

**What to pull, in priority order** — the big-ticket items carry the story:

1. **Garrett G30-770 turbo** + Turbosmart HyperGate45 + TiAL BOV
2. **Haltech S2 + IC7 + harness + fuse box** (likely the single largest line)
3. **KPower swap kit** — adapter plate, flywheel, mounts, oil pan, manifold
4. **Injector Dynamics ID1750XDS + Walbro 450s + fuel system**
5. **Supertech valvetrain + ARP studs + oil pump upgrade**
6. **CSF radiator/oil cooler kit**
7. **EPS conversion kit**
8. Spec Stage 3+ clutch

**Fastest route:** KPower and KMiata order confirmations in Gmail. Search
`from:kpower` and `from:kmiata`, plus PayPal/card statements for Garrett and
Haltech. Most of the total probably sits in 8–10 orders, not 90 receipts.

**Partial is fine.** A segment that says *"the turbo was $X, the ECU and harness
were $Y, the swap kit was $Z, and the remaining ~$8k was sensors, seals and
hardware I stopped counting"* is honest, specific, and better than a precise
number you had to invent.
