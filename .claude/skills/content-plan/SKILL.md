# Content Plan

## Inputs

- `playbook/what-works.md` — bias toward proven structures
- `playbook/hypotheses.md` — every plan should test 1–2 open questions
- `brand/profile.md` — pillar mix and current build state
- Ask what's actually happening in the garage/on track over the window. Real
  events beat invented topics every time.
- Ask realistic available hours. Then plan for about 70% of that.

## Principles

**One shoot, many cuts.** A single wrenching session should yield a long-form
video, 2–3 Shorts, a carousel, and Stories. Filming is the expensive part;
never film once and post once.

**Capture is cheap, plan for it.** The rule is: film everything, decide later.
Missing footage of the moment something broke is unrecoverable.

**Protect the flywheel.** One long-form YouTube per week is more valuable than
seven TikToks, because it's the only asset that keeps earning months later.
If time is short, the long-form survives and the shorts get cut.

**Pillar mix over the window,** roughly: build 40 / racing 25 / money 20 /
life 15. The money pillar is the differentiator — if a plan has zero money
content, that's a flaw, flag it.

**Race weekends are content gold mines and terrible production windows.** Plan
capture-only during the weekend, editing after.

## Output

```
## Content Plan: <date> – <date>

**Context:** <what's happening with the car / calendar>
**Budgeted hours:** <film> filming, <edit> editing

| Date | Platform | Format | Pillar | Working title | Hook | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Shoot days:** <consolidated — what to capture in one session>
**Testing this window:** <which hypothesis, and what result would confirm it>
**Cut first if time runs short:** <ordered list>
```

Always include that last line. A plan without a triage order is a plan that
fails silently when the week goes sideways.
